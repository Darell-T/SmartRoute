# MTA route bullet assets

These static SVG shapes were sourced from `mta-subway-bullets` 1.0.0 and
are rendered through the shared `TrainBullet` component. Their fill colors
were aligned in July 2026 with the MTA's published **MTA Brand Colors / Subway,
SIR & ADA** reference:

https://www.mta.info/document/168976

The route symbols are used for rider wayfinding and do not imply MTA
endorsement. Although the simple symbols may not meet the threshold for
copyright protection, trademark rights may still apply; confirm intended
public/marketing use before release.
